# Changelog

## v1.0 (October 2026)

First public release.

- Registry of 1,574 instruments with triage tiers, families and AGORA crosswalk.
- 770 annotations: 408 Tier A (26 reference, 25 checker-corrected, 357 machine on prompt `1.3-030c54d5`) and 362
  Tier B (light format; prompt `1.3-interim-20261014`, 6 on `1.3-030c54d5`).
- Validation protocol VP-1.0 with two blinded runs (Tier A, Tier B).
- Texts retrieved in October 2026; not distributed.

Planned: human review of the validation checker's verdicts; annotation of remaining Tier A/B instruments whose texts
could not be retrieved; threshold-variable recoding.
